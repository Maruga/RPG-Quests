gothic cartography style, aged parchment texture,

## Variante Più Dettagliata

```
Ancient hand-drawn map of Italian peninsula focusing on 
Emilia-Romagna and Tuscany regions,
six cities marked with elegant gothic symbols:
- One coastal city on Adriatic (east)
- One major city in the north (Milano area)
- One central hub city (Bologna area)  
- Three cities in Tuscany forming a triangle

Parchment background with burn marks and aged stains,
dark ink illustration style, 
thin elegant lines connecting locations like a web,
compass rose in corner, decorative border with thorns or roses,
gothic horror cartography, Renaissance map aesthetic,
no modern elements, no text, mysterious atmosphere
```

## Note

**Città da includere (da nord a sud):**

1. Milano (nord-ovest)
2. Rimini (costa adriatica, est)
3. Bologna (centro-nord, hub)
4. Firenze (Toscana nord)
5. Siena (Toscana centro)
6. Perugia (Umbria)

**Elementi opzionali:**

- Linee di collegamento tra città
- Mare Adriatico a est
- Alpi a nord (accennate)
- Appennini (catena centrale)
- Rosa dei venti
- Bordo decorativo gotico
