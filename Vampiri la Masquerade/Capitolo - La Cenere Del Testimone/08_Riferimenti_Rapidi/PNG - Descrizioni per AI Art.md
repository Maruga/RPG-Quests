## Note per la Generazione

**Stili consigliati da aggiungere:**

- `digital painting, dark fantasy, World of Darkness, Vampire the Masquerade`
- `oil painting, Renaissance chiaroscuro, dramatic lighting`
- `noir photography style, black and white, high contrast`
- `gothic horror illustration, detailed, moody atmosphere`

**Aspetti vampirici opzionali:**

- `pale supernatural skin`
- `subtle fangs visible`
- `predatory aura`
- `unnatural stillness`
- `eyes reflecting light like a cat`
